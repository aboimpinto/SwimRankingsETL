# August–September catch-up — 21 September 2026

Tracking: [SwimRankingsETL issue #1](https://github.com/aboimpinto/SwimRankingsETL/issues/1).

## Coverage and download evidence

Requested window: 1 August through 21 September 2026, all available countries.
The public live index yielded **45 competitions** overlapping that window.
All 45 LENEX downloads succeeded and were validated. The 26 existing files had
identical SHA-256 checksums to the earlier September 15 batch; 19 were new.
No downloaded session date was outside the requested window.

The main site's meet index returned HTTP 403 and its headed desktop browser
remained at security verification. It was closed after reporting the block.
Main-site-only competitions remain unverified. **This is live-index coverage,
not a claim that every competition on SwimRankings was discovered.**

Original files and detailed, uncommitted evidence are retained under
`data/export/catchup-2026-09-21/`: `inventory.json`, `downloads.json` (source IDs,
dates, hashes and sizes), `reconciliation.json`, `dry-run.json`, `imports.json`,
`idempotency.json`, and database/archive before-and-after evidence. Do not publish
raw athlete data, backups or connection files in GitHub.

## Delivery verification

Exported 19 compressed version-1 meet packages (626,581 bytes total). Re-exporting unchanged meets
produced no new revisions. All 19 packages were applied to an isolated PostgreSQL
database using the canonical schema: exactly 19 meets, 8,890 results and 11,291
splits. Reapplying all 19 returned `already_applied` with unchanged counts.
Detailed evidence: `packages-report.json` and `delivery-replay.json` in the local
evidence directory. Synthetic contract tests additionally exercise corrections,
deletions, alias conflicts, target mismatch, partial failures and rollback.

No package was uploaded to AWS. Derived points summaries and active website
caches have not been refreshed. See the [delivery runbook](../docs/desktop-incremental-delivery.md)
for commands, recovery, adoption and the required summary-refresh follow-up.
